4. Registration and authentication

When you create and use a registered account, we process:

  • your full name and email address;
  • an internal user identifier, profile data and account creation time; and
  • authentication and session data required to keep you signed in.

Password authentication is handled by Supabase Auth. We do not receive or store your password in plain text. The data is processed to create your account, authenticate you and assign your application data to your account. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR, insofar as the processing is necessary to provide the service requested by you.

5. Application data

Depending on how you use the application, we process data entered by you, including:

  • contacts: names, email addresses, telephone numbers and colour assignments;
  • tasks: titles, descriptions, due dates, priorities, categories, status and board position;
  • subtasks and their completion status; and
  • assignments between tasks and contacts.

This data is used exclusively to provide the task-management functions. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR where processing is necessary to provide the service requested by you and, where applicable, Article 6(1)(f) GDPR based on our legitimate interest in operating the educational application.

Please do not enter confidential information. Only enter personal data relating to another person if you are permitted to do so and have informed that person where required.

6. Guest access

If you select guest login, Supabase creates an anonymous account with an internal identifier and provides separate demonstration contacts and tasks. Changes made during the guest session are assigned only to that guest account. Guest accounts older than 24 hours are removed by an hourly cleanup process. Deletion therefore generally takes place between 24 and 25 hours after creation and also removes the associated profile, contacts, tasks, subtasks and assignments.

The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is to make the application's functionality available for evaluation without requiring permanent registration.

We use Supabase Pte. Ltd to provide authentication and the PostgreSQL database. Supabase processes data on our behalf as a processor. The primary project data is stored in the Supabase region Central EU (Frankfurt), Germany (eu-central-1). Data is transmitted using encrypted HTTPS connections.

Supabase may use subprocessors to provide its service. Where processing involves a transfer to a country outside the European Economic Area, Supabase provides contractual safeguards, including the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses where applicable. Further information is available in the Supabase Data Processing Addendum.

8. Local storage and cookies

Supabase stores technically necessary authentication and session information in your browser's local storage. This allows an existing session to be restored when you return to the application. The information is removed when you sign out, when the session is invalidated or when you clear the website data in your browser.

We do not use analytics or advertising cookies. Access to local storage is necessary to provide the authentication functionality requested by you. The legal basis for the associated processing is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR and, where applicable, Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. The applicable storage access is technically necessary within the meaning of Section 25(2) TDDDG.

10. Storage periods and deletion

  • Guest accounts and their associated data are generally deleted after 24 to 25 hours.
  • Individual contacts, tasks and subtasks are deleted from the active database when you delete them in the application.
  • Registered account and profile data is stored for as long as the account is used. You may request deletion by contacting us by email. We will also delete the data when the project is discontinued, unless a legal obligation requires longer storage.
  • Hetzner states that Apache access and error logs for web hosting are retained for seven days by default.

Residual copies may remain temporarily in provider backups until the applicable backup cycle expires. Data may be retained for longer only where required by law or necessary for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.
