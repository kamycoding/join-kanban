Data protection at a glance

Join is an educational project. We only process the data you enter yourself — contacts and tasks — and we use it exclusively to make the board work. There is no tracking, no advertising, and no sale of data to third parties.

Which data we store

  • Contact data you create: name, email address, and phone number.
  • Task data you create: title, description, due date, priority, category, and assignees.
  • Technical data required to deliver the application, such as your IP address in server logs.

Where the data is stored

Application data is stored in a Supabase (PostgreSQL) database. Supabase acts as a processor on our behalf. Data is transmitted over an encrypted HTTPS connection.

Legal basis

We process your data on the basis of your consent (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR), which you give by entering data into the application, and on the basis of our legitimate interest in operating a functional application (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR).

Your rights

You have the right to information, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, data portability, and objection regarding your stored data. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at [Your Contact Email].

Deletion

Contacts and tasks are deleted immediately and permanently when you delete them in the application. Because Join is a learning project, please do not enter real personal data or any confidential information.
